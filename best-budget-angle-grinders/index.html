Best budget angle grinders for 2026

Quick answer

The Ryobi PCL500B ONE+ 18V angle grinder is the best budget cordless model for most DIYers thanks to its strong performance and low price.

The Worx WX820L AXIS is the smart pick for occasional light-duty work, while the Milwaukee 2680-20 offers extra power for those already on the M18 platform.

Cordless angle grinders have come a long way. A few years ago, a budget-friendly cordless grinder meant weak batteries and slow cutting. Today, even affordable models can slice through rebar, tile, and metal with surprising ease, and without dragging an extension cord around your job site or garage. The key is finding the right balance of power, runtime, and price for your actual workload. When shopping for a budget cordless angle grinder, pay attention to the battery system you’re already invested in, whether the grinder has a paddle or slide switch for safety, and if it includes a brake (which stops the wheel quickly when you release the trigger). Brushless motors generally give better runtime and longevity, but some brushed models still offer decent value. Best for specific needs

Best for all-around DIY and home projects

If you need one grinder that can handle the occasional metal cut, tile notch, or rust removal without demanding top-tier performance, the Ryobi PCL500B is your tool. Best for occasional light-duty work

When you only reach for a grinder a couple times a year, the Worx WX820L AXIS makes sense because it also works as an oscillating multi-tool for sanding, scraping, and plunge cuts. If you need more torque than the Ryobi offers, for cutting thicker metal or running a wire wheel for hours, the Milwaukee 2680-20 delivers. Our pick: Milwaukee 2680-20.

Our verdict

For most people, the Ryobi PCL500B is the clear winner in the budget cordless angle grinder category: it’s cheap, capable, and easy to live with. If you already own M18 tools and need more muscle, the Milwaukee 2680-20 is a dependable step up. And if your grinding needs are truly occasional, the Worx AXIS gives you two tools in one for very little money. Pick the one that matches your battery system and workload, and you’ll get solid value either way.
