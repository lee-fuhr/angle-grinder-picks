How we choose our picks

We do not run our own timed cut tests or lab measurements. Instead, we build our recommendations by studying long-term owner feedback across forums, retailer reviews, and YouTube teardowns, and by cross-referencing expert reviews from established tool publications. Our focus is on what actually fails or holds up: switch reliability, gear train noise, battery latch breakage, and how the tool performs after a year of real use. We also watch for common complaints like excessive vibration or poor dust sealing. Price is weighed against expected life and battery platform value – a grinder that demands a pricey new battery system may be a worse deal than one that works with batteries you already own. Every pick here has a strong reputation for durability and manufacturer support.
